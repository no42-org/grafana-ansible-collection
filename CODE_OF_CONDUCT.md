# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other interaction in this repository.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissiveness aimed at a person rather than a claim
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Discriminatory language or conduct, including about people's identity or background
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **<ronny@no42.org>**, or privately via GitHub if you would rather not use email.

Reports go to the maintainer, who is one person. Expect a human response rather than a process. Depending on severity the response may be a request to change behaviour, deletion of a comment, or a block — and the maintainer may act without a report if something is obviously out of bounds.

If your complaint is about the maintainer, say so directly; there is no committee to escalate to, which cuts both ways.

## A note on scale

This is a small fork maintained by one person in their own time. This document exists so expectations are written down rather than assumed, not because the project has a conduct problem. Treating it as a serious commitment rather than boilerplate is the point.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), deliberately shortened to what a project this size can honestly promise to enforce.
